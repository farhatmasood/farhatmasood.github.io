3. Operation & Maintenance
Interventional Radiology Failure Analysis (CMH)
  • Problem: Angiography system experiencing HV generator arcing during monsoon season, causing procedure cancellations.
  • Root Cause: Data loggers revealed relative-humidity spikes >75% (spec <60%).
  • Solution & Result: Calculated latent heat load and installed a desiccant dehumidifier (15 L/day), stabilizing RH at 45% (±5%); uptime rose from 78% to 98% over three months.
MRI Helium Optimization
  • Problem & Cause: Excessive helium boil-off; thermal audit identified elevated chiller supply temperature (18 °C vs 15 °C spec) and insufficient loop cooling.
  • Solution & Result: Coupled a dedicated backup process chiller in parallel with automatic switchover and redundant pumps, stabilizing boil-off and reducing unplanned downtime and operating cost.
Accidental MRI Quench (CMH)
  • Problem & Cause: Accidental magnet quench during a high-volume shift; human-factor issues including console layout, single-step quench access and operator fatigue.
  • Solution & Result: Implemented lock-out covers, two-step authorization, CCTV monitoring and structured fatigue/refresher training — zero inadvertent quench events over the following 5 years.
Predictive Maintenance Strategy
  • Problem & Cause: High frequency of breakdown-based interventions; review of 5 years of CMMS data showed recurring failure and age-related wear unaddressed by calendar-based intervals.
  • Solution & Result: Implemented a condition-based maintenance program with evidence-based PM intervals and fault-tree modeling for spares, reducing downtime, emergency call-outs and inventory holding cost.
