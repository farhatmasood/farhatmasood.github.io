Professional Profile

Senior Biomedical & Electrical Engineer with eighteen years of engineering leadership, including a decade of specialised Biomedical and Clinical Engineering practice across a tertiary cardiac institute, a surgical hospital, and a United Nations Level-III field hospital in Sudan. This operational depth is complemented by research in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision and a record of peer-reviewed publications.

My experience spans clinical engineering operations, medical equipment lifecycle management, regulatory compliance (JCI, ISO, IEC 60601), large-scale procurement, and the leadership of multidisciplinary technical teams in resource-constrained environments. Gold Medal recipient (MS Electrical Engineering, 2021), I bridge traditional biomedical engineering with computational healthcare technologies — translating technical depth into operational reliability and patient safety.
