How it works

Two point masses on massless rods. The equations of motion come from the Lagrangian and are solved as a 2 × 2 linear system at every step. Damping adds a torque −γ·Mᵢᵢ·ωᵢ on each joint, so γ is a rate in 1/s. The dissipated energy is integrated alongside, so the energy check (E + dissipated − E₀) stays meaningful with damping.
